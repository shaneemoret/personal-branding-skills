# Design, Trust & Conversion Checklist

Read this for phases 7 and 8. Grade Design and Trust on A–F; grade Conversion Readiness on the readiness scale (Strong / Moderate / Weak / Absent). Design is a business issue whenever it reduces clarity, trust, perceived authority, or conversion — judge it on outcomes, not taste.

## Design & Trust (phase 7)

First impression and visual credibility:

- 5-second clarity — can a first-time visitor tell what this is and who it's for?
- Brand credibility and competitive positioning
- Visual hierarchy and information ordering
- Typography, spacing, and contrast
- Mobile layout, not just mobile rendering
- CTA prominence and visual weight
- Proof visibility: testimonials, logos, case studies, media mentions
- Security/trust indicators where relevant

Output: Design Grade (A–F), Trust Grade (A–F), plus first-impression assessment, hierarchy issues, trust-signal gaps, and mobile-design issues. Route specific problems into Key Findings; the grades go in the Dimension Scorecard.

## Conversion & Commercial Intent (phase 8)

Can a qualified buyer quickly grasp the offer, who it's for, why it matters, why the business is credible, what proof exists, and what to do next?

- Offer clarity
- CTA quality and placement
- Lead capture flow
- Pricing/package clarity where relevant
- Objection handling
- Proof placement relative to the ask
- Contact flow and friction
- Sales-page structure
- Commercial-intent gaps (visitors with buying intent who hit a dead end)

Output: Conversion Readiness on the readiness scale, with specific gaps routed into Key Findings.
