# GEO, Entity, Citation & Authority Signals

Read this for phases 3–6. If you already maintain a `geo-signals.md`, merge these sections rather than overwriting it — the entity, citation, and authority checklists are the newer additions.

## GEO Six-Question Detail (phase 3)

The site should let an AI system answer each with confidence, sourced from on-page content (not inference):

1. **Who is this?** — clear organization identity on the homepage and About page.
2. **What do they do?** — services/products stated plainly, not buried in brand language.
3. **Who is it for?** — named audience or segment.
4. **Why are they credible?** — credentials, track record, named people.
5. **What proof exists?** — outcomes, data, case studies, attributed results.
6. **Which page is safest to cite?** — at least one stable, factual, self-contained page an AI would quote.

Treat `llms.txt` as a secondary signal: note if missing, don't overstate it.

## Entity SEO (phase 4)

Can engines and AI cleanly identify the entity? Grade Entity Strength on the readiness scale.

- Organization, founder/key people, services, products, category, expertise, location, audience
- Social profiles and SameAs references
- About page and founder/person/author pages present and consistent
- Organization schema, Person schema, LocalBusiness schema where relevant
- Brand-name consistency across pages

## Citation Readiness (phase 5)

Can AI safely cite the site? Grade Citation Readiness on the readiness scale.

- Case studies, testimonials, statistics
- Frameworks, methodologies, original insights, research
- Dedicated proof sections
- Outcome claims with source attribution (claims that can't be checked are a liability, not an asset)

## Content Authority (phase 6)

Does the site demonstrate authority in its category? Grade Content Authority on the readiness scale.

- Core topics present vs. missing
- Service-page depth
- Blog/topic clusters and internal linking between related content
- Proof supporting commercial claims
- Buyer-journey coverage
- Balance of informational / commercial / transactional content
