# Thumbnail Reference Examples

Five worked examples showing the strategy-to-execution chain across all five modes.

---

## Example 1 — Executive Authority (Mode 1)

**Input:** "I want a thumbnail for my video about LinkedIn strategy for founders"

**Positioning:** Founders Misuse LinkedIn *(angle: common mistake, not generic advice)*

**Motivation:** Identity + Authority *(founders see themselves; speaker is the expert)*

**Type:** `identity-led`

**Text options generated:**
1. `Founders Misuse LinkedIn` ← chosen
2. `LinkedIn for Founders`
3. `LinkedIn Is Broken`

**Why #1 wins:** It names the audience AND implies a mistake they're making. Creates instant recognition and mild tension.

**Mode:** 1 — Executive Authority
**Dominance:** Text-led

**Variables:**
```
BG_COLOR   #1a1a1a
TEXT_COLOR #C9A84C
FONT       Cormorant Garamond / 500
FONT_SIZE  118px (3 words across 2 lines: "Founders Misuse / LinkedIn")
BLEND_MODE luminosity
TEXT_LEFT  68px  |  TEXT_WIDTH 620px
FACE_TOP   -5%   |  FACE_HEIGHT 115%
```

**Taste notes:** Gold on charcoal — warm, premium, calm. Face blended with luminosity so it reads as part of the dark environment. No subtitle needed.

## Example 2 — Controlled Disruption (Mode 5)

**Input:** "Thumbnail for a video called: Most business coaches are frauds"

**Positioning:** Most Coaches Are Frauds *(the title is already the angle — preserve it)*

**Motivation:** Contrast + Urgency *(provocation + stakes)*

**Type:** `bold-claim`

**Text options generated:**
1. `Coaches Are Frauds` ← chosen
2. `Most Coaches Lie`
3. `The Coaching Scam`

**Why #1 wins:** Most direct. "Coaches Are Frauds" lands harder than hedged versions. No softening.

**Mode:** 5 — Controlled Disruption
**Dominance:** Face-led *(speaker expression carries the claim — leaning in, direct eye contact)*

**Variables:**
```
BG_COLOR   #0a0a0a
TEXT_COLOR #FFFFFF
FONT       Playfair Display / 700
FONT_SIZE  108px
BLEND_MODE normal
TEXT_LEFT  52px  |  TEXT_WIDTH 480px
FACE_TOP   -10%  |  FACE_HEIGHT 125%
```

**Taste notes:** No gold — pure contrast. Face larger and higher. Expression should feel direct, not angry. Still premium. The claim does the emotional work; the face confirms it.

## Example 4 — High-Contrast Authority (Mode 2)

**Input:** "Thumbnail for a video: How to double your consulting rate"

**Positioning:** Double Your Rate *(outcome-first, no filler)*

**Motivation:** Transformation + Urgency *(they want the result, and they want it now)*

**Type:** `bold-claim`

**Text options generated:**
1. `Double Your Rate` ← chosen
2. `Charge More Now`
3. `Your Rate Is Too Low`

**Why #1 wins:** Direct outcome, zero ambiguity. No hedging. The viewer either wants it or they don't — and the ones who do will click immediately.

**Mode:** 2 — High-Contrast Authority
**Dominance:** Text-led *(the number does the work — face is confident, not the hero)*

**Variables:**
```
BG_COLOR    #0a0a0a
TEXT_COLOR  #FFFFFF
FONT_FAMILY Playfair Display
FONT_WEIGHT 700
FONT_SIZE   128px (3 words: "Double Your / Rate")
BLEND_MODE  normal
BRIGHTNESS  1.05
CONTRAST    1.08
TEXT_LEFT   68px  |  TEXT_WIDTH 620px
FACE_TOP    -5%   |  FACE_HEIGHT 115%
```

**Taste notes:** Pure white on near-black — maximum contrast, zero softness. Playfair Display at 700 weight gives it authority without aggression. No gold; gold would soften the urgency. No subtitle — the claim is complete.

## Example 5 — Strategic Tension (Mode 3)

**Input:** "Video about why most people never get promoted — even when they deserve it"

**Positioning:** Why You're Overlooked *(names the feeling, not the topic)*

**Motivation:** Curiosity + Contrast *(viewer suspects this is about them — creates a gap they need to close)*

**Type:** `curiosity-gap`

**Text options generated:**
1. `Why You're Overlooked` ← chosen
2. `The Promotion Gap`
3. `They Won't Promote You`

**Why #1 wins:** "You're" makes it personal and immediate. It implies the viewer is experiencing this right now. Option 3 is stronger in aggression but loses the curiosity gap — it answers instead of opening.

**Mode:** 3 — Strategic Tension
**Dominance:** Face-led *(the expression carries the implication — thoughtful, slightly unsettled, not angry)*

**Variables:**
```
BG_COLOR    #1a1a1a
TEXT_COLOR  #C9A84C
FONT_FAMILY Cormorant Garamond
FONT_WEIGHT 500
FONT_SIZE   115px (3 words across 2 lines: "Why You're / Overlooked")
BLEND_MODE  luminosity
BRIGHTNESS  1.06
CONTRAST    1.10
TEXT_LEFT   52px  |  TEXT_WIDTH 480px
FACE_TOP    -10%  |  FACE_HEIGHT 125%
```

**Taste notes:** Gold on charcoal but with face-led composition — the face is slightly larger than default, creating visual tension that reinforces the psychological hook. More negative space around the text than Mode 1. The asymmetry is intentional: it should feel like something is slightly off, because the content is about something being wrong.
